## 4. 镜头脚本 Shot Sequence Ideas

通用拍摄建议：
- 使用自然光或柔光灯，突出纸张纹理与文字。
- 画面比例 9:16，镜头贴近书页 45° 俯拍或侧拍，配合手指划过重点句。
- 字幕使用高对比无衬线字体（如 Inter、Montserrat），关键句用黄色/白色高亮。
- CTA 统一放在视频最后 3 秒，引导点击 bio 链接或评论关键词。

### Sequence 1：人生上限 / 标准
- **Opening Hook（前 3 秒）**：黑底白字 + 翻页音效：“You don't get what you deserve. You get what you tolerate.”
- **特写页面**：镜头缓慢推近 Chapter 1 页面，手指划过 “Your life's ceiling is defined by what you refuse to reject.”；翻页至解释页，高亮 “You do not get what you deserve; you get exactly what you tolerate.”
- **配音/字幕**：This one line rewired how I set boundaries. If you're tolerating too much, your life will stay small.
- **CTA**：Grab *One True Line* — link in bio. Drop a 🔥 if you're raising your standards.

### Sequence 2：失恋放手 / 自我找回
- **Opening Hook（前 3 秒）**：书页局部模糊渐入，文字逐字浮现：“You didn't lose them. You lost yourself trying to keep them.”
- **特写页面**：Chapter 3 原文页近景，手指点触 “We lose ourselves trying to hold on to people who let go.”；翻页展示解释 “Desperately clinging to someone actively walking away is a guaranteed recipe for losing your own identity.”
- **配音/字幕**：If they already walked away, stop shrinking yourself to fit their absence. This page says everything you needed to hear.
- **CTA**：Save this for the next time your heart tries to hold on. Book link in bio.

### Sequence 3：爱的脆弱 / 信任
- **Opening Hook（前 3 秒）**：书脊缓缓打开，画外音：“Real love is terrifying. Here's why.”
- **特写页面**：Chapter 6 页面 extreme close-up 到 “Love is giving someone the power to destroy you but trusting them not to.”；拉远展示整段解释关于 handing the keys to your vulnerabilities。
- **配音/字幕**：Vulnerability isn't weakness — it's the price of real connection. This one sentence put it into words.
- **CTA**：Tag someone you'd trust with the keys. Get the book — link in bio.

### Sequence 4：社交面具 / 高功能疲惫
